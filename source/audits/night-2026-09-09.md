# The night of 2026-09-09: every unit, what it settled, what it left

**Summary.** Fourteen commits between `174ae9e3` and `2e579f09` changed more of what the port
*is* than any previous cycle: the port made sound, the renderer stopped costing more than a
frame, the in-game clock started running, a played town saved itself and reloaded, and three
separate "port defects" turned out to be the game's own behaviour or another session's
`taskkill`. This page is the index a reader uses to find which unit measured what -- every row
names the commit, the pages it changed, what it SETTLED and what it left OPEN. It is not itself
evidence: each row's citations point at the page that carries the receipts.

**How to read a row.** "Settled" means a claim moved from H to S, E or O, or a standing claim was
retracted with a measurement. "Open" means the unit named the next experiment rather than
finishing it. Where two units disagree, both are listed and the disagreement is a row of its own.

## The disagreement the night left, stated as such

**Which arrival step gates the villagers, and what releases the tutorial hold.** VILLAGER42
measured that with no `X` row in the pad script the Nintendo-DS illustration is still up 11,900
frames later and the pad is discarded, and that `X` then `B` clears it. SAVE43's chain had no `X`
row, the hold released anyway, and villagers were then drawn, walked into and talked to. One run
each way, on different snapshots and different builds
[E: `docs/log/cycle41-gameplay.md` VILLAGER42; `docs/log/cycle42-save.md` SAVE43]. The four-arm
experiment that settles it is written out in `../systems/villagers.md`'s Hypotheses.

## Three port defects that were not

Worth a section of its own, because it is the night's methodological result and it cost three
units to learn.

1. **"No villager is ever drawn"** (GAMEPLAY42) was a REACH problem, then not even that
   [E: VILLAGER42, SAVE43].
2. **"The 4,000-frame picture hold is a port stall"** (cycle41) is the game's own tutorial; the
   original does exactly the same thing on the same frames [O: ORACLE44].
3. **"The town recipe exits 1 -- an AUDIO6 regression"** (RENDER42's observation) was another
   session's `taskkill /IM acww.exe`; the image cannot produce that exit code
   [E: STAB42, `../experiments/run-stability.md`].

The common shape is M1: in each case the instrument, the reach or the environment was the
variable, and in each case the correct next step was to calibrate the measurement rather than to
change the port. **Three separate agents ran `taskkill /IM acww.exe` that night** and each time
killed other sessions' runs -- kill by PID (B14).
